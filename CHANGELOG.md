# Changelog

All notable changes to mdconnect are listed here, newest first. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). The desktop and web apps share one version.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added

- Connect a Sony NetMD device, or a Hi-MD device in NetMD mode, from the desktop app or from Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based browsers.
- Read the disc: its title, groups and tracks, with their full-width titles, recording mode and length, and the time used and left.
- Rename the disc, tracks and groups; create, rename and remove groups; move and delete tracks.
- Play, pause, stop and skip tracks on the device, with the track and position shown as it plays.
- Export the titles to a CSV file and import them back, compatible with Web MiniDisc Pro.
- Wipe the disc.
- Add tracks from audio files in SP, LP2 or LP4, encoded by the built-in encoder or, with your consent, by Sony's encoder online.
- Settings: theme and transparency, full-width title fields on or off, the defaults for adding tracks, and withdrawing consent to the online encoder.
- See what changed in each version in the Changelog, which opens by itself once after an update.
- Links to the MiniDisc guide, the source code and donations in the More menu.
